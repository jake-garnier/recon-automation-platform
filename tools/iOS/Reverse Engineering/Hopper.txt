Hopper
======

URL: https://www.hopperapp.com/

macOS/Linux disassembler and decompiler. Supports x86, x86_64, ARM, and ARM64. Good UI for analyzing macOS and iOS binaries.

Usage: Binary reverse engineering focused on macOS/iOS.
Input: Mach-O, ELF, PE binaries.
Output: Disassembly, pseudo-code, control flow graphs.

Note: Commercial software with free demo mode.
