IDA Pro
=======

URL: https://www.hex-rays.com/products/ida/

Industry-standard interactive disassembler and debugger. Supports analysis of binaries for virtually every processor architecture. Essential for reverse engineering compiled code.

Usage: Binary reverse engineering, malware analysis, vulnerability research.
Input: Binary executables (any architecture).
Output: Disassembly, decompiled pseudocode, analysis.

Note: Commercial software (expensive). Free version (IDA Free) available with limited features.
