Ghidra
======

URL: https://ghidra-sre.org/

NSA's open-source software reverse engineering framework. Disassembler and decompiler supporting many architectures. Free alternative to IDA Pro.

Usage: Binary reverse engineering and analysis.
Input: Binary executables.
Output: Disassembly, decompiled code, analysis.

Note: Free and open-source. Java-based GUI.
