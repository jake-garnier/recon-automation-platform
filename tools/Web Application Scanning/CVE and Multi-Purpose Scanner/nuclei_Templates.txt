nuclei Templates
================

URL: https://github.com/projectdiscovery/nuclei-templates

Official community-curated template library for Nuclei. Thousands of templates covering CVEs, misconfigurations, exposures, takeovers, default credentials, and more.

Usage: Template library for Nuclei scanner.
Categories: CVEs, exposures, misconfigurations, takeovers, default-logins, etc.

Update templates:
  nuclei -update-templates
