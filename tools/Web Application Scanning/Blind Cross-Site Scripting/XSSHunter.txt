XSSHunter
=========

URL: https://github.com/mandatoryprogrammer/xsshunter

Tool for finding blind XSS vulnerabilities. Hosts payloads that phone home when executed, capturing screenshots, cookies, and page content. Self-hosted version.

Usage: Detect blind XSS vulnerabilities.
Input: Inject XSSHunter payloads into target parameters.
Output: Alerts with screenshots, DOM, cookies when payload fires.
