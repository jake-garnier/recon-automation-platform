subjack
=======

URL: https://github.com/haccer/subjack

Subdomain takeover tool written in Go. Tests CNAME records against known vulnerable service fingerprints.

Usage: Detect subdomain takeover vulnerabilities.
Input: List of subdomains.
Output: Vulnerable subdomains.

Example:
  subjack -w subdomains.txt -t 100 -timeout 30 -ssl -a
