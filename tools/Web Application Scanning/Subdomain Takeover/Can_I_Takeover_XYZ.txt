Can I Takeover XYZ
==================

URL: https://github.com/EdOverflow/can-i-take-over-xyz

Community-maintained reference list of services and whether they are vulnerable to subdomain takeover. Not a tool per se, but the definitive reference for takeover fingerprints.

Usage: Reference guide for which services are vulnerable to takeover.
Format: Table of services with vulnerability status, evidence, and documentation.
