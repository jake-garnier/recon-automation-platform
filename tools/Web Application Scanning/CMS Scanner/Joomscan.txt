Joomscan
========

URL: https://github.com/rezasp/joomscan

Joomla vulnerability scanner by OWASP. Detects Joomla version, known vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and installed components.

Usage: Joomla CMS security scanning.
Input: Target Joomla URL.
Output: Version, vulnerabilities, components.

Example:
  perl joomscan.pl -u https://example.com
