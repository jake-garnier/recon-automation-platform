ApkTool
=======

URL: https://bitbucket.org/iBotPeaches/apktool/

Tool for reverse engineering Android APK files. Decodes resources to nearly original form and rebuilds them after modifications. Essential for Android RE.

Usage: Decode/rebuild APK resources, smali code analysis.
Input: APK file.
Output: Decoded resources, smali code, AndroidManifest.xml.

Example:
  apktool d app.apk -o output/
  apktool b output/ -o modified.apk
