DomainTools
===========

URL: https://whois.domaintools.com/

Commercial platform for domain intelligence and reverse WHOIS lookups. Find all domains registered by the same entity using registrant email, name, or organization.

Usage: Reverse WHOIS to discover related domains owned by same entity.
Input: Domain, email, registrant name, or organization.
Output: List of associated domains, WHOIS records.

Note: Paid service, limited free lookups available.
