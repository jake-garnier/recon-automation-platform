naabu
=====

URL: https://github.com/projectdiscovery/naabu

Fast port scanner by ProjectDiscovery written in Go. Designed for reliability and simplicity. Supports SYN/CONNECT scans and integrates with other ProjectDiscovery tools.

Usage: Quick port scanning, often as a precursor to service detection.
Input: Hosts (stdin, file, or CIDR).
Output: Host:port pairs.

Example:
  naabu -host example.com
  naabu -list hosts.txt -top-ports 100
