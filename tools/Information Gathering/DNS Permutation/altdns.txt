altdns
======

URL: https://github.com/infosec-au/altdns

Subdomain discovery through alterations and permutations. Takes existing subdomains and generates mutations (dev, staging, test prefixes, etc.) then resolves them.

Usage: Generate and resolve subdomain permutations.
Input: Known subdomains + words list.
Output: Resolved permuted subdomains.

Example:
  altdns -i subdomains.txt -o permuted.txt -w words.txt -r -s resolved.txt
