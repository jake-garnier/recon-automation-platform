massdns
=======

URL: https://github.com/blechschmidt/massdns

High-performance DNS stub resolver. Capable of resolving billions of DNS records per run using thousands of concurrent lookups. Essential for large-scale subdomain enumeration.

Usage: Mass DNS resolution at scale.
Input: List of domains + list of resolvers.
Output: Resolved DNS records.

Example:
  massdns -r resolvers.txt -t A -o S domains.txt > results.txt
