dnsprobe
========

URL: https://github.com/projectdiscovery/dnsprobe

DNS resolution tool by ProjectDiscovery. Performs multiple DNS queries for a list of subdomains. Fast bulk resolution written in Go.

Usage: Bulk DNS resolution of subdomains.
Input: List of subdomains.
Output: Resolved IPs and DNS records.

Example:
  cat subdomains.txt | dnsprobe
