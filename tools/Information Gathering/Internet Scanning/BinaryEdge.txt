BinaryEdge
==========

URL: https://www.binaryedge.io/

Internet scanning platform similar to Shodan/Censys. Collects data on exposed services, vulnerabilities, and misconfigurations across the internet.

Usage: Search for target infrastructure and exposed services.
Input: IP, domain, or search queries via web UI/API.
Output: Open ports, services, vulnerabilities, data leaks.

Note: Free tier available with limited queries.
