RIPE
====

URL: https://apps.db.ripe.net/db-web-ui/query

Réseaux IP Européens — regional internet registry for Europe, Central Asia, and the Middle East. WHOIS database for IP and ASN lookups.

Usage: IP/ASN ownership lookup for European/Middle Eastern addresses.
Input: IP address, ASN, or organization.
Output: Network allocation, organization, abuse contacts.
