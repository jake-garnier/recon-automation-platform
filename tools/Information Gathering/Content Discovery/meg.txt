meg
===

URL: https://github.com/tomnomnom/meg

Fetch many paths for many hosts without hammering servers. By tomnomnom. Designed for mass content discovery across many hosts while being respectful of rate limits.

Usage: Fetch specific paths across many hosts.
Input: List of hosts + list of paths.
Output: Response bodies saved to files organized by host.

Example:
  meg -d 1000 -v paths.txt hosts.txt output/
