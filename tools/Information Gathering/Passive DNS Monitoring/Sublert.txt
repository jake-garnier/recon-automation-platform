Sublert
=======

URL: https://github.com/yassineaboukir/sublert

Certificate transparency log monitor that alerts on new subdomains. Runs as a cron job and sends notifications (Slack, etc.) when new subdomains appear for monitored domains.

Usage: Continuous monitoring for new subdomains via CT logs.
Input: List of domains to monitor.
Output: Alerts when new subdomains are discovered.
