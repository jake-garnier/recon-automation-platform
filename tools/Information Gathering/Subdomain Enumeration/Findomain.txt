Findomain
=========

URL: https://github.com/Edu4rdSHL/findomain

Cross-platform subdomain enumerator written in Rust. Uses certificate transparency logs and APIs for fast passive enumeration. Supports monitoring mode for continuous subdomain discovery with alerts.

Usage: Fast passive subdomain enumeration and monitoring.
Input: Domain names.
Output: List of subdomains.

Example:
  findomain -t example.com
  findomain -t example.com -o
