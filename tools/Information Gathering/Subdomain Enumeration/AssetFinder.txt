AssetFinder
===========

URL: https://github.com/tomnomnom/assetfinder

Lightweight tool by tomnomnom that finds domains and subdomains related to a given domain. Queries certificate transparency logs, DNS datasets, and web archives.

Usage: Quick passive subdomain/asset discovery.
Input: Domain name (stdin or argument).
Output: Plain text list of related domains.

Example:
  assetfinder example.com
  assetfinder --subs-only example.com
